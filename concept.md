# EVERAFTER · Site concept v1

Electronic music festival, 07–10 Aug 2027, The Valley. Full approved concept (references analysis,
style, block-by-block content and animations) lives in the Claude Doc "EVERAFTER: концепт сайту v1".

## Style
- Dark #0A0A0A, ink #F2EFEA, red #E8321E, orange #FF6A1A, pink #FF3D8B, wine #3A0A0A.
- Archivo variable (wdth 62–125) for display, Geist for text, Geist Mono for HUD labels.
- Duotone stock photos (Unsplash), film grain, WebGL smoke + cursor trail backdrop, custom cursor.

## Blocks
1. Hero: WebGL wordmark that melts under the cursor (spray-paint bleed + RGB split + glitch slices),
   glowing duotone orb, HUD corners, countdown, CTA. Pixel-block preloader.
2. Manifesto: XXL red type, words stretch from condensed to wide on scroll, geometric marks,
   one hand-drawn swash accent, count-up facts, two crossing marquee tapes driven by scroll speed.
3. Lineup: day tabs, horizontal accordion of vertical names, active duotone card, glitch photo swap,
   scramble text on day switch, auto-advance.
4. The world / stages: giant sliding "THE WORLD", strict stages table, cursor-following preview with RGB split.
5. Tickets + footer: sticker tickets fly in and straighten, hover glitch, tear-off stub; footer wordmark.

## Showreel
Open with `?reel` (or leave the mouse idle for ~3 s): the smoke and wordmark follow a virtual cursor,
the lineup and stages tables play through on their own.
